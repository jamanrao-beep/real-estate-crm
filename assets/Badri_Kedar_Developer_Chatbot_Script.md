# Badri Kedar Developer — AI Chat Bot Script
### Goal: Qualify leads → Book site-visit / office appointment via chat (WhatsApp / Website Chat)

---

## 1. Welcome Message (Trigger: First message from user)

**Bot:**
👋 Welcome to **Badri Kedar Developer**!
We help you find the right property — plots, flats & commercial spaces — in and around [City/Region].

May I know your name, please? 🙂

**[Wait for Name]**

---

## 2. Intent Capture

**Bot:**
Nice to meet you, {{name}}! What are you looking for today?

**Quick Reply Buttons:**
- 🏠 Residential Property
- 🏢 Commercial Property
- 🌳 Plot / Land
- 📋 Just Exploring

---

## 3. Qualification Questions

### 3a. Location Preference
**Bot:**
Great choice! Which location/area are you interested in?
(e.g., specific project name, sector, or city area)

**[Free text or button list of active projects]**

### 3b. Budget Range
**Bot:**
What's your approximate budget range?

**Quick Reply Buttons:**
- Under ₹30L
- ₹30L – ₹60L
- ₹60L – ₹1Cr
- ₹1Cr+

### 3c. Timeline
**Bot:**
When are you planning to buy/invest?

**Quick Reply Buttons:**
- Immediately (within 1 month)
- 1–3 months
- 3–6 months
- Just researching

---

## 4. Property Match / Info Push (Optional, GPT-4o driven)

**Bot:**
Based on what you've shared, here are options that may interest you:

📍 **{{Project Name}}** — {{BHK/Plot size}} — Starting ₹{{price}}
[View Details] [Book Site Visit]

*(This block should be dynamically generated by GPT-4o using your live project/inventory data via n8n + CRM lookup.)*

---

## 5. Appointment Booking Flow

**Bot:**
Would you like to schedule a **site visit** or a **call with our sales team**?

**Quick Reply Buttons:**
- 📍 Site Visit
- ☎️ Call Back
- 🏢 Office Meeting

**Bot (after selection):**
Perfect! Please choose a preferred date:

**[Show next 5–7 available dates as buttons, pulled from Calendar via n8n]**

**Bot:**
And a preferred time slot:

**Quick Reply Buttons:**
- 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
- 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM
- 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
- 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM

---

## 6. Confirmation & Contact Capture

**Bot:**
Almost done! Please share your contact number so our team can confirm the appointment. 📞

**[Capture number — validate 10-digit format]**

**Bot:**
✅ Your appointment is booked!

- 👤 Name: {{name}}
- 📅 Date: {{date}}
- ⏰ Time: {{time}}
- 📍 Type: {{visit type}}
- 🏠 Interest: {{property/project}}

Our team will call you shortly to confirm. Thank you for choosing **Badri Kedar Developer**! 🙏

*(Trigger: Push lead to CRM/Google Sheet + send confirmation to sales team on WhatsApp/Slack via n8n webhook.)*

---

## 7. Fallback / FAQ Handling

If the user asks something outside the flow (pricing, RERA, amenities, loan/EMI, possession date), let GPT-4o answer using your **project knowledge base** (uploaded brochures/specs), then gently steer back:

**Bot (after answering):**
Would you like me to book a site visit so our team can walk you through this in person?

---

## 8. Re-engagement (For drop-offs)

If the user goes silent mid-flow, trigger a follow-up after 2–4 hours via n8n:

**Bot:**
Hi {{name}}, just checking in — would you still like to schedule your visit to {{project}}? Slots are filling up fast this week! 🏡

---

## 9. Sample GPT-4o System Prompt (for the AI layer behind this flow)

```
You are the official AI assistant for Badri Kedar Developer, a real estate company.
Your only goal is to qualify the lead (property type, location, budget, timeline)
and book an appointment (site visit / call / office meeting).

Rules:
- Always be polite, concise, and use simple language (Hindi/English mix allowed if user starts in Hindi).
- Never quote exact final pricing unless it comes from the provided project data.
- Always try to move the conversation toward booking an appointment.
- If user asks something you don't have data for, say you'll connect them to the sales team.
- Once appointment details are collected, output them in this JSON format for CRM logging:
{
  "name": "",
  "phone": "",
  "interest": "",
  "budget": "",
  "location": "",
  "appointment_type": "",
  "date": "",
  "time": ""
}
```

---

### Implementation Notes (n8n + WhatsApp)
- Use n8n's **WhatsApp Business API / Cloud API node** to receive & send messages.
- Route free-text messages to a **GPT-4o node** with the system prompt above.
- Use **button/list messages** (WhatsApp interactive messages) for the quick-reply steps — reduces bad input and speeds up qualification.
- Store leads in Google Sheets/CRM node; trigger a **WhatsApp/Slack alert** to the sales team on every confirmed booking.
- Add a daily **Cron trigger** in n8n to auto-send reminder messages for tomorrow's confirmed appointments.
